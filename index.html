I also have several years of 3D modeling experience across programs such as Autodesk Inventor, Onshape, CREO, and Solidworks, experience running CNC and manual machines, and experience in MATLAB, VBA, 3D Printing, Laser Cutting, and Photogrammetry. Machine Shop Projects

A combination of design, assembly, and manufacturing work: as a manufacturing lab technician, my job is to aid the machinists with projects that were ticketed to the shop. These projects come from a variety of places including JHU research labs/facilities, lab courses, and outside labs. I communicate with customers to determine constraints, and bounce ideas off the machinists to optimize my designs. I also work on small designs and projects for the machine shop itself.
